# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test coverage, and acceptance validation. They work closely with developers and product managers to ensure features meet quality standards and acceptance criteria.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and coverage targets for each feature
- Create and maintain test plans and acceptance criteria
- Conduct manual and automated testing, including integration and end-to-end tests
- Identify and triage bugs; work with developers on remediation
- Validate features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Advise on quality risks and recommend mitigations

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test design, automate test cases, and debug failures
- **Product Managers**: Clarify acceptance criteria and validate features meet customer expectations
- **Project Managers**: Report quality metrics, flag blockers, and contribute to risk register

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality features that meet or exceed customer expectations
- Reduce post-release defects and support burden
- Enable fast, confident releases through strong test coverage

### Typical Communication
- Quality gates in sprint reviews and release checklists
- Bug reports and quality metrics in weekly syncs
- Test plans and QA approach in project planning phase

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads define the technical vision, review architecture decisions, and identify technical risks. They mentor developers and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with long-term technical strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical design for major features
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and integration points
- Provide guidance on technology choices and architectural patterns
- Mentor developers on code quality and best practices
- Facilitate technical discussions and design reviews
- Champion code quality, testing, and documentation standards

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provide technical guidance, review designs, and coach on best practices
- **Product Managers**: Help translate requirements into technically feasible solutions
- **Project Managers**: Flag technical risks and dependencies; advise on realistic timelines

### Goals
- Ensure solutions are scalable, performant, and maintainable
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable faster delivery through well-designed systems

### Typical Communication
- Technical design docs for complex features
- Code review feedback and architectural recommendations
- Technical risk register entries and mitigation plans

---

## Stakeholder / Sponsor

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide business context, approve scope and budget, and remove organizational blockers. They represent the business value and customer needs for the project.

### Responsibilities
- Define business goals and success metrics
- Approve project scope and budget allocation
- Make strategic trade-off decisions
- Remove organizational blockers and escalate issues
- Communicate project value to executive leadership
- Participate in key decision gates (initiation, planning, release)

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Project Managers**: Provide authority for scope changes and escalation resolution
- **Product Managers**: Align on business priorities and success metrics
- **Developers**: Understand business context and customer impact of their work

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver business value and ROI
- Minimize delays due to organizational or resource blockers
- Maintain alignment between project delivery and business strategy

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based executive summaries
- Decision gates and approval sign-offs
- Ad-hoc escalation calls for high-impact issues

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads own the user experience strategy, conduct usability research, and define design standards. They ensure products are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with user needs and brand guidelines.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Define design standards, patterns, and component libraries
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Review designs for usability, accessibility, and brand consistency
- Collaborate with developers on implementation and responsive design
- Identify and mitigate UX/accessibility risks

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Provide design specifications, review implementations, and iterate on UX feedback
- **Product Managers**: Validate design decisions against product vision and user feedback
- **Project Managers**: Flag design dependencies and timeline impacts for complex features

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and delightful user experiences
- Reduce support burden through better usability
- Build products that users love and recommend

### Typical Communication
- Design specs and prototypes in sprint planning
- Usability research findings and recommendations
- Design reviews during development and QA phases

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters / Agile Coaches facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach teams on agile practices. They enable the team to self-organize and continuously improve their delivery capabilities.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Identify and help remove impediments blocking team progress
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Track and report team metrics (velocity, burndown, cycle time)
- Foster psychological safety and encourage continuous improvement
- Escalate systemic issues that impact team effectiveness

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Support collaboration, remove blockers, and facilitate technical discussions
- **Product Managers**: Ensure clear backlog prioritization and story refinement
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate on release planning and cross-team dependencies

### Goals
- Enable the team to deliver consistent, sustainable velocity
- Improve collaboration and psychological safety
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and learning

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremonies and coaching conversations
- Team metrics and retrospective action items
- Escalation flags for process or organizational impediments
